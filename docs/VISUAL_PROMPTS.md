# Tilt World — visual target prompts

Gameplay design targets, generated **before** scene/UI code. They are not captures of the built game. One ChatGPT chat, one image per prompt, landscape 16:9. Record exact prompt, chat URL, attempts and hash in `docs/visual-targets/PROMPTS.md`.

Shared style line (append to every prompt): *Polished premium 3D indie game look, like Super Mario Galaxy meets Outer Wilds and Solar Ash: a tiny jewel-box planet with real 3D depth, curved horizon, crisp lighting, rim light, soft bloom. NOT flat vector art, NOT a worksheet. Hebrew right-to-left game HUD with chunky rounded panels, Rubik-like font, readable at a glance. Exciting, something a 13-year-old would want to play.*

## 01-first-sunrise.png — the first 30 seconds

Generate an image, landscape 16:9: an in-game screenshot of a polished 3D browser game called "Tilt World" (Hebrew title עולם נטוי). Third-person chase camera slightly above and behind a small cute solar-powered photographer robot named Tzila: a rounded white-and-coral robot body, one huge camera lens for a face with a glowing iris, two folding solar-panel wings on her back, little antenna, stubby legs, a camera strap. She runs across the night side of a tiny planet Earth whose horizon curves sharply (the planet is small, like Mario Galaxy). Real continents visible on the surface, dark blue ocean with faint specular glints, warm yellow city lights dotting the night-side land, a lat/long grid faintly glowing. Ahead of her, on the right (east), a crisp glowing orange-pink dawn line, the terminator, sweeps across the planet's surface toward her; beyond it the day side is bright. Deep starfield with a small Moon. A speed trail behind the robot. Hebrew RTL HUD: top right a rounded panel with the level name 'שלב 1: הזריחה הראשונה' and three empty stars; top left round pause and mute buttons; a small round compass/mini-globe with N up and an arrow 'מזרח →' pointing right; a battery icon blinking low red; a world clock showing '04:40' with a sun icon; a single tutorial line in a speech bubble 'השחר מגיע ממזרח. רוצי לקראתו!'; bottom right a big round camera shutter button. [+ shared style line]

## 02-midnight-sun.png — later, World 2: orbit dial in use

Same game, same art style, landscape 16:9: an in-game screenshot from World 2. Tzila stands with her tripod on a snowy hill in Lapland beside a cozy red wooden cabin, far north on the tiny planet; it is midnight and the Sun hangs low but ABOVE the horizon, painting everything gold, long shadows; a curious polar bear peeks from behind a snowy rock. The planet's tilted axis is visible as a faint line through the north pole. Picture-in-picture in the top-left corner: a round inset view from space showing the Sun in the centre and the Earth on its orbit at the June position, the axis tilted 23.5° toward the Sun, the northern cap fully lit. Bottom centre: a chunky circular 'orbit dial' widget with month ticks and the label '21 ביוני' on it, two arrow buttons, and a small counter 'סיבובי חוגה: 2/3'. Top right: level name 'שלב 8: שמש חצות' and stars; world clock reading '00:00' with a sun-above-horizon icon; a photo order card 'צלמי: השמש מעל האופק בחצות'. A viewfinder frame overlay centred on the Sun with the camera shutter button glowing ready. [+ shared style line]

## 04-eclipse.png — World 4: Moon dial and totality
