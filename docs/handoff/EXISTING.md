The sims closest to this round's games, and their verbs:
- **SeasonTiltOrbitLab** (3D): drag Earth around the Sun or click a solstice/equinox station; heat gauges.
- **DayNightSpinExplorer**: drag the Earth disk to spin it; cities light up by day/night.
- **MoonPhaseTideLab**: drag the Moon around the Earth; phase and tide readout.
- **AlbedoBalanceLab**: paint surface tiles (snow, desert, forest, asphalt) and read the temperature.
- **ClimatographDetectiveLab**: identify a climate zone from a climograph; builder mode.
- **SeaBreezeLab**: drag the land's heat up and watch the high/low and the breeze.
- **IsraelRainTransectLab**: move a marker along a transect of Israel, set altitude and distance from the sea, read the yearly rain (800 mm, 200–350 mm, <100 mm challenges).
- **AtmosphereLayersLab**: a rocket probe through the layers; match phenomenon cards to layers.
- **PlateBoundaryLab**: pick a boundary (מתרחקים/מתנגשים/מחליקים) and the crust types (land–land, ocean–land); the landform morphs.
- **EarthLayersDrillLab**: a depth slider drills down; heat/pressure gauges and a material-state badge.
- **RockCycleExplorer**: a cycle wheel; click a process to move a rock between igneous, sedimentary, metamorphic and magma.
- **EarthquakeReadinessLab**: Japan, Haiti, Israel; a preparedness budget, then run a quake and read the damage.
- **IsraelLandformBeltsLab**: drag process chips (deposition, folding, faulting, volcanism, weathering, salt) onto the belt cross-section.
- **IsraelLandscapeMixerLab**: dials for altitude, rain and distance from the sea turn into a landscape.
- **RiverFlowSculptorLab**, **WeatheringErosionLab**: sculpt river flow, erosion processes.
